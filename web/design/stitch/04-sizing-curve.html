info TANGEDCO LT-1A Telescopic Slab Dynamics

Under Tamil Nadu Net Billing rules, every unit consumed behind the meter offsets grid power priced at upper domestic slabs (up to Rs 8.00 - Rs 11.00/kWh). Once your daytime solar generation exceeds your instantaneous demand, surplus energy flows back into the grid at the mandated Feed-in Tariff (approx Rs 2.28/kWh).
