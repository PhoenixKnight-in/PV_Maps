Grid-connection and regulatory disclosures

Net-metering / Net-billing approval from TANGEDCO

Physical roof capacity does not automatically grant grid connectivity. Commercial power export requires an approved feasibility sanction and certified bi-directional meter synchronization directly distributed by TANGEDCO engineers.

Sanctioned-load ceiling

Under Tamil Nadu regulatory framework, grid solar interconnection is strictly capped by your registered domestic sanctioned service load (currently 3.0 kW). System sizing above this limit requires an explicit prior load enhancement application.

Sanctioned load escalation notice

Raising your sanctioned service load with TANGEDCO is an independent administrative process. It carries distinct development charges, security deposit fees, incoming wire gauge compliance checks, and local distribution transformer headroom verification. It is not bundled within standard turnkey vendor installation contracts.

TNERC Net Billing compliance note

Under the active consumer tariff order, excess power exported to the utility grid is credited at the designated statutory Feed-in Tariff (approximately ₹ 2.28 / kWh) rather than offset against the full telescopic retail tariff tier.
